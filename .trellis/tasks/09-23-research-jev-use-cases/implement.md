### Decision tree

1. First user-visible outcome (settled: A+C, trustworthy structured Job information and less repetitive cleanup).
2. Automation boundary (settled): daily Jev may auto-project stable high-confidence
   existing Skills; every unsafe outcome stays Candidate/unresolved. Source records
   remain intact.
3. Maintenance authority (settled): stronger model auto-applies high-confidence
   existing mapping/alias/generic/reject; new Skills/hierarchy require one batch
   confirmation.
4. Maintenance trigger (settled): default 30-day eligibility check, minimum 50
   eligible exceptions, plus manual run-now; all Settings-adjustable and empty
   checks make no provider request.
5. Quality policy (settled): no manual gold-label project; confidence is an
   operational routing signal, not measured accuracy. Budgets, thresholds and
   frozen snapshots are visible and adjustable.

Confirmed domain distinctions are recorded in `CONTEXT.md`: a Job Evidence Finding concerns source support, and a Suspected Duplicate Association relates separately retained records without confirming that they are one vacancy.
